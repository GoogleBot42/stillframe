DynamicFrame

E-ink picture frame firmware, built with ESPHome. Install it from this page over USB — no toolchain, no command line.

You need Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Installing uses Web Serial, which Safari and mobile browsers do not support. Connect the board over USB before you start.

How it goes

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Pick your device above and press Install, then choose the board's serial port in the browser dialog.
  3. When flashing finishes, the same dialog offers Connect to Wi-Fi. Enter your network there — the credentials go straight to the device over USB.
  4. Open Home Assistant. The frame appears under Settings → Devices & Services as a discovered ESPHome device; click Configure to adopt it.

Updates are automatic. Once installed, each frame checks this site for new firmware and installs it over Wi-Fi on its own. You only need the USB cable once.

Notes

The frame spends nearly all of its time in deep sleep, waking on a timer to pull a fresh image and again to look for firmware updates. If you need it to stay awake — to check logs, say — hold the wake button on boot, or flip the Prevent Deep Sleep switch in Home Assistant before its next wake-up.

Images come from a DynamicFrame server on your own network, which crops, resizes and dithers pictures for the panel. Point the device at it from Home Assistant after adopting.
